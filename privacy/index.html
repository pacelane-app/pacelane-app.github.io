隱私權政策 Privacy Policy

適用 App：PaceLane（iOS）　最後更新：2026 年 11 月 10 日

簡單說

  • 不需要帳號。你的跑者資料、課表和跑步紀錄只存在你的手機裡，不會上傳給我們（我們沒有自己的伺服器）。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有分析工具、不追蹤你。
  • 位置只在你開啟「GPS 記錄距離」並開始跑步時，在手機上計算距離和配速，不會傳出手機。
  • 訂閱由 Apple 處理付款，App 透過 RevenueCat 確認訂閱狀態。

存在你手機裡的資料

跑步經驗、比賽與目標、可訓練天數、課表、每日狀況回報、跑步紀錄、跑鞋里程和設定。刪除 App 會一起刪除，也可以在「個人 › 刪除所有資料」清除。匯出 CSV 時，檔案只會傳到你自己選擇的地方。

位置

只有在你開啟 GPS 記錄並按下開始時，App 才會在前景使用位置來計算距離與配速。位置資料存在你的手機上，不會傳給我們或任何第三方。不開 GPS 也能使用計時和手動記錄。

通知

訓練提醒是在你的手機上排定的本機通知，不經過任何伺服器。

訂閱（Apple、RevenueCat）

PaceLane Pro 透過 Apple 的 App 內購買訂閱，付款由 Apple 處理，我們看不到你的付款資訊。App 使用 RevenueCat, Inc. 的服務確認訂閱是否有效，過程中會處理一組匿名的 App 使用者識別碼、Apple 購買收據和基本的 App／裝置資訊，不包含你的姓名、電子郵件或訓練資料。詳見 RevenueCat 隱私權政策 與 Apple 隱私權政策。

健康聲明

PaceLane 提供一般性的訓練建議，不是醫療建議。開始新的訓練計畫前請先諮詢醫師，身體不適時請停止跑步並就醫。

兒童

本 App 不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計，我們也不會蓄意收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更與聯絡

若本政策有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方日期。有任何問題請寄信到 pericprew@gmail.com。

App: PaceLane (iOS) · Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short

  • No account. Your runner profile, plans and runs are stored only on your iPhone and are never uploaded to us (we have no servers).
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Location is used only while you record a run with GPS turned on, on your device, to measure distance and pace. It never leaves your phone.
  • Apple processes subscription payments; the app checks your subscription status through RevenueCat.

Data on your device

Experience, races and goals, training days, plans, daily check-ins, runs, shoe mileage and settings. Deleting the app deletes them, or use Profile › Delete all data. A CSV export goes only where you choose to share it.

Location

Only when you turn on GPS tracking and start a run, the app uses your location in the foreground to calculate distance and pace. It is stored on your device and is not sent to us or any third party. Timing and manual logging work without GPS.

Notifications

Training reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; no server is involved.

Subscriptions (Apple, RevenueCat)

PaceLane Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple In-App Purchase; Apple handles payment and we never see your payment details. The app uses RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm whether your subscription is active, which processes an anonymous app user ID, Apple purchase receipts and basic app/device information — not your name, email or training data. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Health

PaceLane gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before starting a new training programme, and stop running and seek help if you feel unwell.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions: pericprew@gmail.com.

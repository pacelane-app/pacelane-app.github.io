PaceLane

PaceLane 是你的馬拉松訓練教練。依照你的跑步經驗、最近的跑量、每週能練的天數和比賽日期，排出 5K、10K、半馬、全馬到 50K 的每週課表，再陪你每天照著練。

PaceLane is your marathon training coach for iPhone: week-by-week plans for 5K to 50K built around your running, your schedule and your race day.
